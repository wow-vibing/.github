# Code of conduct

We are a small group building game-modding tools together. We expect everyone to:

- be respectful and constructive: critique code and designs, not people;
- welcome questions and newcomers; explain decisions instead of asserting them;
- respect other people's work: no deleting, force-pushing over or silently reverting someone else's changes;
- keep private things private: credentials, personal data and members-only content stay inside the organization;
- respect the rights of others: no distribution of copyrighted game files or assets you don't have rights to.

Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks are not tolerated. Report problems privately to an organization owner;
reports are handled confidentially. Owners may warn, suspend or remove members who break this code.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
